# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

tack is a project for everyone. We want participating here — in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space — to be a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for all, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most people are trying to help.
- Be patient with newcomers. Everyone was a beginner once.
- Give feedback on the code, not the person. Be specific and constructive.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and be willing to change your mind.
- Respect people's time: write clear issues with reproductions, and keep discussions on topic.
- Credit others for their work and ideas.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately inflammatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information (such as a physical or email address) without explicit permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after maintainers have made a decision.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, and when someone officially represents the project in public spaces (for example, posting from an official account or acting as a representative at an event).

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers privately at **ekdanai.kk@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Reporters will not face retaliation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including:

1. **Correction** — a private, written note explaining what was inappropriate.
2. **Warning** — a formal warning with consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary ban** — no interaction with the project for a set period.
4. **Permanent ban** — for serious or repeated violations.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may be removed from the project.
